What is HOSA?

A student-led organization that promotes careers in health care.

HOSA – Future Health Professionals brings students together to learn about the health care field while building teamwork and leadership skills.

Members take part at the middle school, secondary, and post-secondary or collegiate level, guided by four core values. Members can enter HOSA's competitive events, apply for scholarships awarded at the International Leadership Conference, and use HOSA's internship and job resources.

Learn

Explore health professions and build knowledge beyond the classroom.

Lead

Develop the leadership and teamwork skills health care needs.

Serve

Give back to the communities health professionals work in.

Innovate

Bring new ideas to how care is delivered and understood.

Rhode Island HOSA

Our state association.

2017Rhode Island became an affiliated HOSA state in June.
11Secondary chapters across the state.
3Conferences each year: fall, state, and international.

Rhode Island started with two secondary chapters and has grown since. The State Advisor is Rachel Roberge.

State conferences are open to all members and are held at Johnson & Wales University in Providence. Students who medal at the State Leadership Conference qualify for the International Leadership Conference in June.
